    How play data works
    • name is required. focus is a one-line summary. attack is "ours" or "opp".
    • frames is the list of steps, at least two. Step 1 gives a spot to every player in the play. Later steps list only the players who move.
    • Players are "1" to "15" for our team and "o1" to "o15" for the opposition.
    • Spots are [across, along] in meters. Across: 0 is the left touchline, 70 the right. Along: 0 is the try line we attack, 22 their 22, 50 halfway, 100 our own try line.
    • ball is a player who carries it, like "9", or a spot on the ground, like [35, 50]. Step 1 needs one. Add "kick": true to a step to show the ball in the air.
    • Passes must go backwards. When the ball changes hands, the catcher must be at least 1m behind the passer, or the play won\u2019t load. Add "kick": true or "throw": true (lineout) to steps that aren\u2019t passes.
    • cap is the coaching caption for that step. roles gives each of our players a job, shown when someone taps them.
    • Optional: cat puts the play in another tab (attack, defense, breakdown, setpiece). Otherwise it goes in Team plays. The pitch zooms to fit the players on its own.
    • One file can hold several plays as a list. Saving a play with the same name replaces the old one.
